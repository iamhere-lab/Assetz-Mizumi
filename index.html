Frequently Asked Questions

What homes are available at Assetz Mizumi Reserve?

3 and 4 BHK luxury apartments in three layouts: a 3 BHK + 3T of 1,901 sq ft, a larger 3 BHK + 3T of 2,165 sq ft and a 4 BHK + 4T of 2,476 sq ft (super built-up areas). Carpet areas including balcony are 1,431, 1,648 and 1,869 sq ft.

What is the starting price?

Approximate all-inclusive prices start at ₹2.56 Cr* for the 1,901 sq ft 3 BHK, ₹3.05 Cr* for the 2,165 sq ft 3 BHK and ₹3.57 Cr* for the 2,476 sq ft 4 BHK. Prices are indicative; ask for the cost sheet for the exact cost of a unit.

Where is Assetz Mizumi Reserve located?

In Kudlu, off Hosur Road, Bengaluru 560068. The Kudlu Gate and Hosa Road stations on the Yellow Line are about 1 km away, and Hosur Road, the Outer Ring Road and Sarjapur Road are 15 to 20 minutes from the project.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. The RERA registration number is PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/040226/008450.

What amenities does the project have?

30+ curated amenities, with a clubhouse of about 63,000 sq ft in each phase. They include a swimming pool and kids' pool, gym, yoga room, badminton, squash, tennis, padel and pickleball courts, box cricket, a bowling alley, banquet hall, amphitheatre, work stations, a crèche and a pets' park.

What makes the homes different?

About 75% carpet area efficiency (RERA carpet plus balcony), terrace balconies of up to 170 sq ft, walk-in and tucked-in wardrobes, and a domestic help room with its own entry in the 4 BHK. About 77% of the site is open space, beside two natural lakes.

Who is the developer?

Assetz Property Group, with 50+ residential and commercial projects, 50+ million sq ft developed and ongoing, and 24,000+ units delivered and underway.
